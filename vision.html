সোনার বাংলাBuilding Sonar Bangla in office.

The campaign is over; the government's record is now the story. Here's what the Adhikari ministry has done since taking the oath on 9 May 2026.

First moves

The opening weeks

At its first cabinet meeting, the new government moved to bring several central schemes into the state and to signal a change in administrative direction.

Central schemes

Ayushman Bharat & PM-JAY

The government announced the rollout of Ayushman Bharat and the PM Jan Arogya Yojana health coverage scheme in the state.

Education

PM SHRI schools

Adoption of the PM SHRI schools initiative was announced alongside the other central scheme rollouts.

Livelihoods

Vishwakarma & Ujjwala

The Vishwakarma Scheme for traditional artisans and the Ujjwala Yojana for LPG connections were both brought into the state's implementation plan.

Social welfare

Beti Bachao, Beti Padhao

The girl-child welfare and education scheme was added to the state's programme list in the government's opening moves.

Governance

Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita alignment

The administration signalled it would align state processes with the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, India's revised criminal code.

Civil service

Retirement age & IAS training

The government raised the state government job age limit by five years and moved to increase IAS officers' participation in central training programmes.

Since 9 May 2026

Two early, defining decisions

Two announcements in particular have drawn attention as signals of governing style.

Language

Bengali for government work

The Adhikari government announced that Bengali would be mandatory for state government work from September 2026, framed as a commitment to the state's own language and identity.

Land

A direct-purchase approach to land

Citing the memory of Nandigram and Singur, the government announced a policy of direct land purchase for industrial projects rather than compulsory acquisition — a deliberate break from the disputes that first made Adhikari's name, and a promise not to repeat them.

These are policy announcements as reported at the time of writing; implementation details and timelines should be confirmed against official state notifications before publishing.
