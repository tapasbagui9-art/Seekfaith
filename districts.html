Home ground

Purba Medinipur: where it started

Adhikari's own political base sits in Purba Medinipur — home to Karkuli, his birthplace, Nandigram, the constituency and movement that defined his early career, and Kanthi (Contai), the seat long associated with the Adhikari family in public life.

2007

The Nandigram movement

Adhikari led local resistance to a proposed land acquisition for a chemical hub, an episode that first brought him to statewide attention and gave Nandigram its lasting place in Bengal's political memory.

2021 & 2026

Nandigram, twice over

He won the Nandigram assembly seat in both the 2021 and 2026 elections — defeating Mamata Banerjee in 2021, and defeating his own former aide, Pabitra Kar, in 2026.
