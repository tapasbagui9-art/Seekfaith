A leader who was told the road back would take a generation did it in six years — one booth, one district, one karyakarta at a time.

Born on 15 December 1970 in Karkuli, Purba Medinipur, Suvendu Adhikari first drew statewide attention leading the 2007 Nandigram land-acquisition movement. He went on to serve as a two-term MP from Tamluk and a cabinet minister in the state government, before a decision in December 2020 reset Bengal's political map entirely.

On 9 May 2026, he took the oath as the ninth Chief Minister of West Bengal — the first from the Bharatiya Janata Party — closing one campaign and opening the next: building Sonar Bangla in office.
